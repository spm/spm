# Research impact bibliography: studies that report use of SPM 25

This bibliography accompanies the research impact statement in the SPM 26 paper. It lists 76 publications and software records, including 41 journal articles, reporting use of SPM 25 (Tierney et al., 2025, https://doi.org/10.21105/joss.08103).

On 13 October 2026, we searched for records published since January 2025 that cited the SPM 25 paper in OpenAlex or Semantic Scholar, or mentioned "SPM25", "SPM 25", "SPM 25.01", "spm-python" or the paper's DOI in full text indexed by Europe PMC or OpenAlex. This yielded 171 candidates. We included records only when the authors reported using SPM 25 in their own analyses or developing software based on it. Preprints and their published versions were counted once.
